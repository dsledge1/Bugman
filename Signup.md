Household Calendar SMS Signup
---

Household Calendar is a private SMS calendar assistant for invited household members. It provides conversational calendar assistance, event confirmations, updates, and reminders.

By texting START to (980) 825-4986, you agree to receive recurring conversational household calendar assistance, event confirmations, updates, and reminders from Household Calendar. Message frequency varies based on your requests and calendar activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
