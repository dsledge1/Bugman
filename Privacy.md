#Privacy Policy
---

This private household calendar service collects users’ mobile phone numbers, message contents, and calendar-related requests to provide requested SMS assistance and notifications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers acting solely to deliver the messaging and calendar services.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Twilio and Google solely to operate the service. Users may reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance.
